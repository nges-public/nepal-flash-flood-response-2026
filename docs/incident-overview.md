# 2026 Nepal Flash Flood Incident Overview

## Background

On 26 August 2026, a catastrophic flash flood triggered by a high-altitude glacial collapse struck central Nepal, causing widespread destruction across the Bhote Koshi–Trishuli river corridor. This disaster resulted in significant damage to settlements, transport infrastructure, hydropower facilities, and public services across multiple districts (Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Chitwan, Gorkha and Tanahun), prompting large-scale rescue, relief and humanitarian response efforts. 

## Purpose of this Repository

This repository was established by the Nepal Geomatics Engineering Society (NGES) to coordinate volunteer GIS efforts supporting rescue, relief and early recovery operations.
The primary objective is to produce standardized, openly accessible geospatial datasets for humanitarian response.

## Mapping Objectives

- Map temporary holding centres and evacuation shelters.
- Digitize affected road segments and access routes.
- Record landslide and flood impact locations.
- Maintain validated spatial datasets for responders.

## Geographic Scope

Affected districts include:
- Rasuwa
- Nuwakot
- Dhading
- Chitwan
- Gorkha
- Tanahun

## Data Principles

- Open humanitarian data
- Standardized schema
- Community validation
- No personally identifiable information (PII)
